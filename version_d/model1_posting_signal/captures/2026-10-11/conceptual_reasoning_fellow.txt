About Anthropic

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want
AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly
growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders
working together to build beneficial AI systems.



Program Overview
Many of the questions that will shape our future have no single checkable answer.
Reasoning well about them, whether in law, policy, philosophy, economics, cognitive
science, or AI safety itself, takes the kind of judgment experts spend years building. We
believe frontier AI, developed safely, can become a real partner on these questions, but
getting there means working closely with the people who know these fields best.

This fellowship brings researchers and experts from philosophy, cognitive science,
economics, policy, and law into Anthropic for four months to work alongside our
research teams. Fellows pair deep domain expertise with our frontier models and internal
tooling to evaluate, improve, and apply Claude's capabilities in their field.
