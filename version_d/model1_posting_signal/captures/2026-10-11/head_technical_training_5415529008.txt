About the role

The world's engineers want to get the most out of Claude. At Anthropic, technical
instruction already happens everywhere: our Applied AI and Forward Deployed
Engineering teams teach customers and partners, and engineers across the company help
others learn to build with Claude. Today that instruction is a shared responsibility,
delivered alongside people's primary roles. As demand grows, we need to turn that
distributed expertise into a dedicated technical instruction function.

You will build that function.

You will be the first person at Anthropic whose full-time job is technical instruction. You'll
spend your first year primarily in the classroom teaching engineers, architects, and other
technical practitioners. You'll also build the people, standards, and systems that let
Anthropic's technical instruction scale far beyond the people we directly employ.

This is a player-coach role. From day one you manage a small bench of instructors. You set
the bar for what "ready to teach" means, decide who is cleared to teach a given module in
Anthropic's technical programs, and assess facilitators from our training partners against
that bar.

You're an engineer who teaches and a teacher who has led other teachers. You can build an
agent live in front of a skeptical engineer, diagnose what isn't working in a technical lab,
and make the material better. You can also watch an experienced instructor teach, tell
them their session isn't ready yet, and coach them to get there.

You will report to the Head of Technical Enablement. The role is based in San Francisco
with some travel expected.


Key responsibilities

     Lead from the front. Teach hands-on sessions to new Applied AI engineers, our field
     teams, and partner cohorts, in rooms from 12-person labs to 100-person training
     days.
     Build the instructor bench. Hire and develop instructors and curriculum
     developers. Decide contract renewals, and recommend conversions to full-time,
     against criteria you define.
     Set the quality bar. Own the observation rubric, dry-run protocol, and cleared-to-
     teach process for every instructor-led technical session.
     Scale through train-the-trainer. Set the bar the Lead Technical Instructor's train-
     the-trainer program teaches to, for internal experts, contract instructors, and partner
     facilitators.
     Turn expertise into instruction. Turn internal experts' content into sessions and
     labs that work in a live room.
     Enable programs without owning them. Sta" other teams' programs with cleared
     instructors, and own the quality of instruction inside them.
     Measure and improve. Report learner pass rates per instructor each month, and act
     on them.
